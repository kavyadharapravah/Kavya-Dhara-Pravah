Payal Gaming

Payal Dhare, widely known across the digital and esports world as Payal Gaming, is one of India’s most prominent female gaming content creators, live streamers, and social media influencers. Born on September 18, 2000, in Umranala, a small village in the Chhindwara district of Madhya Pradesh, she grew up in a modest middle-class family alongside her parents, Shiv Shankar and Sangeeta Dhare, and her two sisters, Anjali and Bhumi. Her rise from a small town to becoming the face of female gaming in India is widely celebrated as a milestone in the country's creator economy. Payal’s entry into gaming was largely accidental. While pursuing a Bachelor of Commerce degree at Rungta College in Bhilai, Chhattisgarh, she was introduced to PUBG Mobile by her college friends. What began as a casual pastime quickly turned into a passion. In March 2019, she launched her YouTube channel, initially uploading gameplay videos under the name Substance Payal. Although her early uploads received modest views, her persistence paid off when she began live streaming in September 2020. Viewers were immediately drawn to her energetic commentary, humorous reactions, and authentic engagement with her chat. Her career trajectory accelerated dramatically when she joined S8UL Esports in July 2021, aligning herself with India’s premier gaming and content organization alongside top creators like Mortal and Scout. Just a month later, in August 2021, she made history by becoming the first Indian female gaming streamer to surpass one million subscribers on YouTube, earning the coveted Gold Play Button. She continued breaking barriers by crossing three million subscribers in 2023 and eventually building a massive community of over 4.6 million subscribers on YouTube and more than five million followers on Instagram.
