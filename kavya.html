Kavya Dhara Pravah

Kavya Dhara Pravah (@kavyadharapravah) is a dedicated Hindi literary and poetry community page on Facebook that functions as an active digital platform, or Sahityik Manch, for writers, poets, and literature enthusiasts across India. Created to preserve, nurture, and promote the richness of the Hindi language in the digital era, the page brings together both emerging voices and seasoned literary figures on a shared virtual stage. Its primary focus revolves around organizing regular online Kavi Sammelans (poetry gatherings) through Facebook Live, with the community having successfully hosted well over 140 distinct editions of these live literary sessions. During these broadcasts, typically scheduled in the evenings around 9:00 PM IST to maximize audience engagement, participating poets recite original compositions spanning diverse traditional and modern genres such as Kavita (free-verse and metered poetry), Geet (lyrical songs), Ghazals, Muktak, and Dohas. Beyond routine weekly or bi-weekly recitations, Kavya Dhara Pravah frequently curates special themed episodes tied to major Indian festivals, seasonal transitions, and national occasions, including dedicated Sawan specials, Krishna Janmashtami celebrations, Kajliyan gatherings, and patriotic tributes. Each event is promoted in advance on the page's timeline using custom digital flyers that introduce the featured lineup of poets, session moderators (Sanchalak), and guest speakers, allowing followers to tune in and interact in real time through comments and live reactions. A defining aspect of the page's community-building approach is its commitment to encouraging budding writers by formally recognizing their contributions; after participating in live sessions or literary contests, poets are routinely awarded digital certificates of appreciation (Samman Patra), which are also shared publicly on the page to celebrate their milestones. In addition to video streams, the timeline features written poetry snippets, literary quotes, event announcements, and highlights from past broadcasts, creating an accessible archive for anyone looking to enjoy Hindi verse or connect with fellow creators. Overall, the Kavya Dhara Pravah Facebook Page stands out as a welcoming, grassroots literary network that bridges geographical distances and keeps the oral tradition of Hindi Kavi Sammelans thriving online.
